Privacy Policy for My Money Log

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Developer: Sushil Yadav (yadavsushil.com.np)

Summary: My Money Log is an offline-first personal finance tracker. Your financial transaction records stay securely on your personal device.

1. Information We Store

All expense, income, category, and loan entries you create are stored locally on your device storage (via local SQLite / IndexedDB). We do not collect, read, or sell your financial transactions or personal entries. We do not have access to the data you enter.

2. Biometric Authentication

If you enable fingerprint or screen lock inside the app, authentication is handled entirely by your device hardware operating system. The app never accesses or stores your biometric credentials.

3. Cloud Backup & Internet Access

Internet access is only utilized if you choose to trigger cloud backup, check for app updates, or open external links. When signed in for cloud backup, your data is securely stored under your account and can be deleted by using the app's delete account feature or by contacting us.

4. Contact

For any questions or support regarding this policy, contact Sushil Yadav at: sushilraey@gmail.com.
